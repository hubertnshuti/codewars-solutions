<h2><a href=https://www.codewars.com/kata/657e578bdc80170abd4dca79/train/typescript target="_blank">Minimum Percentage  of Visitors that Ate All Foods</a></h2><h3>7 kyu</h3><br><h1 id="task">Task</h1><p>You are the manager of an amusement park! Today you are doing research on the revenue from various food items sold throughout your venue. All the vendors working for you can report the percentage of visitors that eat that particular food that they sell. Based on this data, your current goal is to create a method to figure out the <em>minimum percentage</em> of all visitors that must have purchased an item from all of the available vendors in your park.<br><br></p><h1 id="examples">Examples</h1><ol><li><p>Imagine the park only had one food (<code>Popcorn</code>). If <code>76%</code> of the visitors get popcorn, then the answer is obvious:<br>a minimum of <code>76%</code> definitely ate every item because there is only one existing item to purchase.</p></li><li><p>If the park had two items (<code>Hot Dogs</code> and <code>Cotton Candy</code>) and <code>50%</code> of the visitors ate a hot dog and <code>50%</code> of the visitors had some cotton candy, it would be easy to say that the <em>maximum</em> percentage of visitors that ate both items would be <code>50%</code>. But you are trying to find the <em>minimum</em> percentage, and it turns out that would actually be <code>0%</code> of visitors that ate both items, which is maybe not so obvious.</p></li><li><p>In another example with a larger menu of now four food items available:</p><ul><li>Donuts    <code>65%</code></li><li>Pretzel   <code>80%</code></li><li>Pizza     <code>80%</code></li><li>Ice Cream <code>90%</code></li></ul><p> the result would be that <code>15%</code> of all visitors ate every one of the foods.</p></li></ol><p><br><br></p><h1 id="input">Input</h1><p>An (<code>array</code>, <code>list</code>, <code>tuple</code>, or <code>vector</code>) of integers (with length from <code>1</code> to <code>10</code> inclusive).<br>Each number represents the percentage (from <code>0</code> to <code>100</code> inclusive) of visitors that ate a particular food item.<br><br></p><h1 id="output">Output</h1><p>The minimum percentage of visitors that must have eaten all foods.
