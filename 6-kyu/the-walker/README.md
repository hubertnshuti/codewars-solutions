<h2><a href=https://www.codewars.com/kata/5b40b666dfb4291ad9000049/train/typescript target="_blank">The Walker</a></h2><h3>6 kyu</h3><p><img alt="alternative text" src="https://i.imgur.com/5YNemou.png?1"></p><p>The walker</p><p>The walker starts from point O, walks along OA, AB and BC. When he is in C (C will be in the upper half-plane), what is the distance <code>CO</code>? What is the angle <code>tOC</code> in positive degrees, minutes, seconds?</p><p>Angle <code>tOA</code> is <code>alpha</code> (here 45 degrees), angle <code>hAB</code> is <code>beta</code> (here 30 degrees), angle <code>uBC</code> is <code>gamma</code>(here 60 degrees).</p><h4 id="task">Task</h4><p><code>function solve(a, b, c, alpha, beta, gamma)</code> with parameters</p><ul><li>a, b, c: positive integers in units of distance (stand for OA, AB, BC)</li><li>alpha, beta, gamma: positive integers in degrees (positive angles are anticlockwise)</li></ul><p>returns an array:</p><ul><li>first element: distance CO (rounded to the nearest integer)</li><li>then angle tOC with the third following elements:</li><li>second element of the array: number of degrees in angle tOC (truncated positive integer) </li><li>third element of the array: number of minutes in angle tOC (truncated positive integer)</li><li>fourth element of the array: number of seconds in angle tOC (truncated positive integer)</li></ul><h4 id="example">Example:</h4><pre><code>print(solve(12, 20, 18, 45, 30, 60)) -&gt; [15, 135, 49, 18]- CO is 14.661... rounded to 15- angle tOC is 135.821...so- degrees = 135- minutes = 49.308...- seconds = 18.518...hence [15, 135, 49, 18]</code></pre><h4 id="note">Note</h4><p>If you need the constant <code>pi</code> you can use <code>pi = 3.14159265358979323846</code></p>
